ANCESTOR DINNER — OPENING PRESENTATION SCRIPT
November 12, 2026 · i.c.stars
Organized by Mariia Kit, Raymond Hall and Andre Woolfolk

EVERY STORY BEGINS SOMEWHERE.
Good evening, everyone, and welcome to Ancestor Dinner. Tonight is about more than sharing a meal. It is about remembering the people who came before us, celebrating the traditions we carry, and learning from one another. Every person at this gathering brings a story.

OUR ROOTS SHAPE OUR JOURNEYS.
Our ancestors faced different circumstances. Some made sacrifices, built homes, changed countries, raised families, or passed down values. We carry those stories forward in our own choices. Take a moment to think of someone who helped you become the person you are today.

FOOD IS A FORM OF MEMORY.
Think of a meal you remember from childhood. You may remember the smell, the table, or the person who made it before you remember the recipe. Traditions survive because people take the time to share them.

DIFFERENT TRADITIONS. THE SAME LOVE.
Among us are people with African American, Mexican, Pakistani, Ukrainian, and Belizean roots, and many other stories. Tonight’s menu includes tamales, Ukrainian dumplings, deruny and syrnyky, collard greens and cornbread, flan, and many more family favorites. We will not assume what each dish means to its contributor. We invite each person to tell us in their own words.

WE GROW STRONGER TOGETHER.
At i.c.stars, we bring different skills, experiences, cultures, and perspectives to one community. Working together does not require us to be the same. We learn from one another, support one another, and work toward a shared purpose.

A MEMORY WORTH KEEPING.
Before dinner begins, we invite you to take part in a small activity called The Roots We Carry. There are cards and pens beside a wooden Memory Box. Think about a value, a lesson, a wish, or a memory passed down through your family or by someone who shaped you. Write as much or as little as you like, then place your card in the box at any point during the evening. You may leave it anonymous. Sharing is entirely optional. We will keep these messages as a reminder of our gathering, and will only publish words online if their authors give permission.

EVERY STORY BELONGS AT OUR TABLE.
Thank you to everyone who prepared food, helped organize this gathering, or brought their presence and curiosity. As we enjoy dinner, ask someone about their dish. Who taught them? What memory does it hold? You are welcome to share only what you feel comfortable sharing. And if you wish, add a message to our Memory Box during the evening. Thank you, and welcome to our table.


UPDATED KEEPSAKE PRESENTATION (SLIDES 6–8)

SLIDE 6 — THE UNITY GARLAND
Tonight, we invite each of you to add one wooden bead to our Unity Garland. Each bead represents one person, one journey, one story. By joining them on a single thread, we create a reminder that our differences are valuable and our connection makes us stronger. Add a bead whenever you are ready.

SLIDE 7 — THE ROOTS WE CARRY MEMORY BOX
We also invite you to write one value, lesson, wish, or memory from your family or ancestors on a card and place it in our wooden Memory Box. The garland represents our connection; the box preserves the wisdom we carry. You may write anonymously, and taking part is optional. We will only share messages publicly with permission.

SLIDE 8 — WELCOME TO OUR TABLE
Thank you for being here and bringing your stories, traditions, and food. We are different people with a shared purpose. Please enjoy the meal and the chance to learn about one another. Welcome to our table.
