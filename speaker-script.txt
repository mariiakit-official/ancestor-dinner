Before Guests Arrive

-   Laptop connected to the projector; website open; Notes closed on screen.

-   Memory Box set out with cards and pens.

-   Beads and cord kept together for the closing (enough cord for about 20 beads and
    secure knots).

-   Candles, vase and flowers on the table; the Empty Chair in place.

-   Each speaker has this printed script or it open on a phone.

Part 1 · Opening Presentation

Before dinner · click “Opening” on the website · about 5–7 minutes

MARIIA KIT · Slides 1–3

Slide 1 · Every Story Begins Somewhere

On screen: candles

  [Pause to let the candle image settle.]

Good evening, everyone, and welcome to our Ancestor Dinner.

Tonight, we’re doing more than sharing a meal. We are honoring the people who came before
us, the stories that shaped our families, and the traditions we continue to carry.

Every one of us brings something from the past — a memory, a lesson, a value, or the
courage of someone who made sacrifices for the next generation.

Tonight, we make space for those stories and for one another.

Slide 2 · Our Roots

On screen: wheat

  [Advance to the wheat image.]

Wheat represents roots, growth, and the traditions passed through generations. From a
small seed, something can grow that nourishes many people.

Our journeys have not been the same. Some of our families have been in one place for
generations; others have crossed borders or begun again. But each of us has been shaped by
those who came before us.

Think for a moment about someone who gave you a lesson or a value that you still carry.

Slide 3 · The Empty Chair

On screen: empty chair

  [Pause briefly — let the silence hold for 3–5 seconds.]

This empty chair reminds us of the people who cannot be here with us today, but whose
love, wisdom, and courage still shape our lives.

Their chair may be empty, but their stories still have a place at our table.

  [Turn to Raymond.]

  Handoff: “Raymond will share how those stories live on through the traditions we carry.”

RAYMOND HALL · Slides 4–6

Slide 4 · The Traditions We Carry

On screen: family recipe

Thank you, Mariia.

Some of our strongest memories begin with a smell from the kitchen, a family gathering, or
a dish we watched someone prepare when we were young.

Food can hold a memory of home. Sometimes a recipe carries a story that words cannot tell.

Slide 5 · Different Cultures, Shared Values

On screen: food

Tonight, our table reflects African American, Mexican, Pakistani, Ukrainian, and Belizean
roots, among others.

People have planned to bring tamales, Ukrainian dumplings, deruny and syrnyky, collard
greens with cornbread, salmon croquettes, flan, and other family favorites.

But the most meaningful part is not a label on a dish; it’s the person’s story. As we eat,
ask someone why they brought what they brought. They may tell you about a family
tradition, a childhood memory, or something they hope to pass on.

Slide 6 · One Shared Purpose

On screen: community

At i.c.stars, we come together with different backgrounds, experiences, and skills. We
don’t all think or live alike, and that’s a strength.

We can listen to one another, support each other, and work toward a purpose bigger than
ourselves.

We do not need the same roots to grow together.

  [Turn to Andre.]

  Handoff: “Andre will introduce a way to preserve some of the wisdom we bring tonight.”

ANDRE WOOLFOLK · Slides 7–8

Slide 7 · The Roots We Carry: Memory Box

On screen: memory box

Thank you, Raymond.

At this table you’ll see our wooden Memory Box, along with cards and pens. We invite you
to write one value, lesson, memory, or wish that comes from your family or ancestors —
something you want to carry into the future.

You can sign your name or leave your card anonymous. Fold your card and place it in the
box whenever you’re ready during dinner. Participation is completely optional, and we
won’t read or publish anyone’s words without their permission.

This is a quiet moment to reflect. Later this evening, we’ll come back together for one
more activity — the Unity Garland.

Slide 8 · Welcome to Our Table

On screen: welcome

Thank you to everyone who cooked, helped organize, or simply came ready to share tonight.

As we enjoy dinner, please ask one another about the food and the traditions behind it,
and share only what feels comfortable.

On behalf of Mariia, Raymond, and me, welcome to Ancestor Dinner.

Many Roots. One Table. One Purpose. Enjoy the meal!

Part 2 · During Dinner

-   Guests may put Memory Box cards in at any point during dinner.

-   Encourage conversations about the dishes and the stories behind them.

-   Do not read handwritten messages aloud or post them without the authors’ permission.

Part 3 · Closing: Unity Garland

Near the end of the evening · click “Closing activity” on the website

  [Have the beads, cord, and Memory Box ready. Ask guests to gather around a table.]

RAYMOND HALL · Closing Slide 1

Closing 1 · Connected by Our Stories

On screen: unity garland

Earlier tonight we remembered our ancestors and the traditions we carry. Now we’d like to
create something together.

This is our Unity Garland. Each of us can add one wooden bead to the cord. Every bead is
unique, just like every person and every story in this room.

Together, those beads become one connected piece. There is no need to give a speech, and
participating is always your choice.

  [Invite everyone to add a bead. Andre helps pass the beads; Mariia keeps the cord
  steady. Allow about 10 minutes.]

MARIIA KIT · Closing Slide 2

Closing 2 · One Purpose

On screen: community

  [When the last bead has been added, secure the cord.]

We began this evening by remembering the roots that shaped us. We end by making a
connection together.

Each bead is different, but each has a place on this one cord. That’s the kind of
community we are building at i.c.stars.

The Memory Box holds the lessons and values we carry. The Unity Garland shows the
connection we have created together.

ANDRE WOOLFOLK · Final Thank-You

Thank you for sharing your food, your stories, and your time with us. We hope tonight
leaves you with a new connection and a memory worth keeping.

Many Roots. One Table. One Purpose.

  [Place the completed garland next to the Memory Box for a photograph. Check everyone’s
  comfort before sharing identifiable photos online.]
